# Richy Events Lagos — Website Overview
### A Plain-English Guide for Non-Developers

---

## 1. What Is This Website?

The Richy Events Lagos website is an online showroom and booking platform for event equipment rentals. It allows potential customers to:

- Browse all available equipment with photos and prices
- Add multiple items to a quote cart
- Send a complete rental request directly to WhatsApp
- View past event photos in a gallery
- Learn why Richy Events is the right choice

Think of it like an online shop — but instead of buying, customers are requesting a rental quote that gets sent straight to your WhatsApp.

There is also a "Load from Cart" button at the top of this section — this pulls in whatever items the customer already added to their cart (from the equipment cards above) and pre-fills the form for them.

## 3. The Quick Quote Cart (Shopping Cart Feature)

This is one of the most important features on the website. Here is how it works:

**Step 1:** Customer browses the equipment and clicks "Add to Quote" on any item they like.

**Step 2:** A small notification pops up at the top of the screen confirming the item was added (e.g. "Chiavari Chairs added to quote!").

**Step 3:** The purple/pink cart button at the bottom-right shows a red badge with the number of items added.

**Step 4:** Customer clicks the cart button to open the cart panel. Inside they can:
- See all selected items
- Increase or decrease quantities using + and − buttons
- Remove individual items using the trash icon
- See the running daily total in Naira
- Click "Send to WhatsApp" to send the full quote
- Click "Clear Cart" to start over

**Step 5:** When "Send to WhatsApp" is clicked, WhatsApp opens with a pre-written professional message that includes all items, quantities, prices, and the total.

The cart also remembers items even if the customer refreshes or leaves the page and comes back.

## 7. How a Customer Books (Step-by-Step)

**Option A — Quick Cart Method (Fastest)**
1. Browse equipment and click "Add to Quote" on desired items
2. Click the purple cart button (bottom-right)
3. Adjust quantities if needed
4. Click "Send to WhatsApp"
5. WhatsApp opens with a complete quote message — customer just hits send

**Option B — Detailed Form Method**
1. Scroll to the "Quick Quote Request" section
2. Tick the equipment needed and set quantities
3. Add any extra items in the "Additional Items" field
4. Fill in name, phone, event date, and number of days
5. Click "Send to WhatsApp"
6. WhatsApp opens with a full detailed quote including customer info
