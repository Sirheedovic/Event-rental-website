### BEFORE ❌

```
Customer Journey (OLD):

1. See Chiavari Chairs
   ↓
2. Click "Book via WhatsApp"
   ↓
3. WhatsApp opens
   ↓
4. Send message for chairs
   ↓
5. Go back to website
   ↓
6. See Round Tables
   ↓
7. Click "Book via WhatsApp" AGAIN
   ↓
8. WhatsApp opens AGAIN
   ↓
9. Send ANOTHER message
   ↓
10. Repeat for each item... 😫

Result: Multiple messages, tedious process
```

---

### AFTER ✅

```
Customer Journey (NEW):

1. Browse all equipment
   ↓
2. Click "Add to Quote" on items they like
   ↓
3. See cart badge update (visual feedback)
   ↓
4. Review cart when ready
   ↓
5. Adjust quantities if needed
   ↓
6. Click "Send to WhatsApp" ONCE
   ↓
7. Professional quote sent! 🎉

Result: One message, easy process
```
